Step by step

Setting up the Procore integration

A one-time setup by an administrator, then it runs on its own every day.

1
One-time setup

Create a Procore integration app

In your Procore account, generate OAuth credentials (a client ID and secret) for a data connection. You'll also note your Procore company ID.

2
One-time setup

Connect it in ShiftWork

Open Integrations → Procore in the ShiftWork admin, paste your company ID, client ID, and secret, then click Test Connection to confirm ShiftWork can reach Procore.

3
One-time setup

Map locations to projects

For each ShiftWork location, enter its matching Procore project ID. Only mapped locations sync — so you can roll out project by project at your own pace.

4
Optional

Set up cost codes

Add your cost codes in ShiftWork and link them to Procore. Skip this if you only need overall daily manpower — you can add it later.

5
One-time setup

Turn on auto-push

Flip Enable integration and Auto-push on submit. From now on, submitting a daily report sends its manpower to Procore automatically.

6
Every day

Your crew just works

Employees clock in and out as usual on the kiosk or mobile app. ShiftWork keeps a running, verified tally of hours and headcount per site.

7
Every day

Submit the daily report

At the end of the day a supervisor submits each site's daily report. That single action locks the numbers and triggers the push to Procore.

8
Every day

Confirm in Procore

The manpower appears in the project's daily log. ShiftWork records the sync status, so you always know it went through — and can re-push in one click if you ever need to.

Good to know

Procore integration questions

Do I need a Procore account already?

Yes. The integration connects your existing Procore account to ShiftWork. You'll create OAuth credentials in Procore and paste them into ShiftWork once.

Does this handle Procore timesheets too?

By default it populates the daily log manpower — total workers and hours per project, per day. If you also want per-employee detail, you can switch on timesheet sync, and ShiftWork will push each worker's hours to Procore as timecard entries. It's an optional toggle you can turn on or off at any time.

What if a location isn't mapped to a project?

Nothing is sent for it. Only locations you've mapped to a Procore project ID sync, so unmapped sites are simply skipped until you're ready.

Can I push a day manually?

Yes. Beyond the automatic push on submit, an administrator can re-sync any daily report on demand — handy if a project was mapped after the fact.

Is my Procore login safe?

The connection uses Procore's OAuth 2.0. Credentials live at the company level in ShiftWork, are never shown to field employees, and can be disabled at any time.

How much does the integration cost?

The Procore integration is part of ShiftWork's Enterprise plan for multi-project operations. Talk to our team and we'll walk you through the fit for your setup.
