Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

How does photo and GPS verification work?

When an employee clocks in or out — at the kiosk or on their phone — ShiftWork captures a photo via the camera and the device's GPS coordinates. Both are stored with the time record, so every punch has visual and location proof attached.

Do employees need smartphones?

No. The kiosk mode turns any tablet or computer with a webcam into a shared time clock with PIN verification. The mobile app is optional and great for distributed crews, but never required.

Can I ask custom questions at clock-out?

Yes. Administrators can configure custom clock-out questions — like "Did you get injured today?" — and answers are stored with each shift for compliance and reporting.

How does PTO tracking work?

You configure an accrual rate and starting balance per employee. ShiftWork accrues hours monthly, deducts automatically when time off is approved, and shows managers the balance — with warnings — right in the approval screen.

What happens when someone calls in sick?

The shift opens automatically, and ShiftWork shows you ranked replacement candidates filtered by availability and conflicts. Notify them by push, SMS, or email with one click, and assign whoever accepts.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are fully running within a day: add your people and sites, set PINs, place a tablet at the entrance, and publish your first schedule.
