Privacy Policy

Effective date: [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]] · Last updated: [[LAST_UPDATED]]

This Privacy Policy explains how [[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]] (“Loqzen”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares personal information when you use the Loqzen website, web app, mobile app and kiosk app (together, the “Service”).

1. Who is responsible for your information

Loqzen is used by businesses (“Customers”) to schedule and track the time of their workers. When a Customer adds you as an employee, the Customer decides what information is collected about you and how it is used, and Loqzen processes that information on the Customer’s behalf. For questions about your employer’s use of your data, contact your employer first. For information about Customer account administrators and website visitors, Loqzen is responsible.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and contact details: name, email address, phone number, role, company, and login credentials (handled by our authentication provider).
  • Work and time records: schedules, shifts, clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, time-off requests, timesheets, daily reports and answers to clock-in questions.
  • Location: precise device location only at the moment you clock in or out in the mobile app, so your employer can verify the job site. We do not track location in the background.
  • Photos: clock-in photos and photos you attach to daily reports or documents.
  • Kiosk PIN: a PIN used to identify you on a shared kiosk device.
  • Device data: device identifiers and a push-notification token so we can send you shift and schedule notifications.
  • Billing data (Customers): subscription and payment information, processed by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Website data: information you submit in a demo or contact form (name, company, email, phone, message).

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: scheduling, time tracking, timesheets, reports and notifications.
  • To verify attendance with location and photos when the Customer has enabled those features.
  • To send account emails such as invitations and password resets, and optional SMS or push notifications.
  • To process subscriptions and payments.
  • To connect with third-party tools the Customer enables, such as Procore.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To respond to support, sales and demo requests.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Service providers we share information with

We share information only with providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that limit their use of it:

  • Authentication: Google Firebase.
  • File and photo storage: Amazon Web Services (S3).
  • Payments: Stripe.
  • Email delivery: [[EMAIL_PROVIDER]].
  • SMS (if enabled): Twilio.
  • Push notifications: Expo and Apple / Google push services.
  • Hosting and database: [[HOSTING_PROVIDER]].
  • Integrations the Customer turns on, such as Procore.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, with notice where required.

5. How long we keep information

We keep Customer data for as long as the Customer’s account is active and then for [[DATA_RETENTION_PERIOD]], unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example, payroll and time records).

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each Customer’s data is kept separate from other Customers’ data. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected parties of a breach as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can update your profile in the app, change the app language, and turn off push notifications in your device settings.
  • You can deny location or camera permission; some clock-in features may then be unavailable if your employer requires them.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information. To make a request, contact your employer or email us at [[PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL]]. To request deletion of your account, use [[ACCOUNT_DELETION_URL]].

8. Children

The Service is intended for workers of working age and is not directed to children under [[MINIMUM_AGE]].
